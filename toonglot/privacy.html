Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Toonglot translates comics on your device. It is built so that what you read stays with you.

What stays on your device

  • The comic pages you open, the text Toonglot reads from them and their translations are processed on your device with Apple's Vision, Translation and, where available, Apple Intelligence. They are never sent to the developer or to any translation server.
  • Your library, reading positions, favorites, history, glossaries, fixes and settings are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is on, they are also kept in your own private iCloud account, which the developer cannot read.

What Toonglot doesn't do

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • The developer collects no data from the app.

Network connections

  • The built-in browser loads the websites you visit, as any browser does. Those sites receive your requests and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • Comic images are downloaded straight from the site that hosts them.
  • Apple downloads translation language packs and Apple Intelligence models, and handles purchases through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy.

Diagnostics

  • Toonglot keeps a reading log on your device: chapter titles, website names, how long each step took and memory use, with no images or text from the comics. It leaves your device only if you share it yourself from Settings.
  • If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash and energy reports.

Children

Toonglot is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will appear here with a new date.

Contact

Use the App Support link on Toonglot's App Store page.

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 8 日

通格 在你的裝置上翻譯漫畫，設計原則是：你讀的東西只留在你身邊。

留在裝置上的資料

  • 你打開的漫畫頁面、從中讀到的文字和翻譯，都在裝置上用 Apple 的 Vision、翻譯與（支援的裝置上）Apple Intelligence 處理，從不傳給開發者或任何翻譯伺服器。
  • 書庫、閱讀進度、我的最愛、瀏覽紀錄、詞彙表、修正與設定都存在裝置上。開啟 iCloud 同步時，也會存在你自己的私人 iCloud 帳號，開發者無法讀取。

通格 不做的事

  • 沒有帳號，不用登入。
  • 沒有分析、廣告或追蹤程式庫，不販售也不分享任何資料。
  • 開發者不從 App 收集任何資料。

網路連線

  • 內建瀏覽器會載入你造訪的網站，和一般瀏覽器一樣；這些網站會收到你的請求，並依它們自己的隱私權政策設定 Cookie。
  • 漫畫圖片直接從存放它的網站下載。
  • 翻譯語言包、Apple Intelligence 模型由 Apple 下載，購買由 App Store 處理，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。

診斷資料

  • 通格 在裝置上保留閱讀紀錄：章節標題、網站名稱、各步驟耗時與記憶體用量，不含漫畫的圖片或文字。只有你自己從「設定」分享時才會離開裝置。
  • 如果你在裝置設定中選擇與 App 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 可能將匿名的當機與耗電報告提供給開發者。

兒童

通格 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。

變更

本政策如有變更，會在此更新並標示新日期。

聯絡

請使用 通格 在 App Store 頁面上的「App 支援」連結。
