Toonglot 通格

Support

Something not working, or a translation that keeps coming out wrong? Open an issue and describe the site (no need to share comic pages), your device and what you expected.

Purchases

Restore a purchase in Settings → Toonglot Pro → Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Translation

Download source languages in Settings → Source Language. Tap any bubble to see what was read and to fix it in your own words; fixes are remembered for the series.

Battery

Settings → Performance → Light uses the least battery: no Apple Intelligence polishing and no AI artwork repair.

支援

遇到問題，或某個翻譯一直錯？請開一則 issue，說明網站（不需要附漫畫頁面）、你的裝置，以及你預期的結果。

購買

在「設定」→「通格 Pro」→「恢復購買」恢復購買紀錄；在裝置的「設定」→「Apple 帳號」→「訂閱項目」管理或取消訂閱。退款由 Apple 處理：reportaproblem.apple.com。

翻譯

在「設定」→「來源語言」下載語言。點任何對話框，可以看讀到的原文，並用自己的話修正；修正會記住整部作品。

耗電

「設定」→「效能」→「輕量」最省電：不使用 Apple Intelligence 潤飾與 AI 補圖。
