Otterflow

Scroll on. It's already in your language.

Otterflow translates foreign-language comics on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Open a comic page, tap Read Translated, and keep scrolling: speech bubbles are erased and re-lettered in Traditional Chinese, entirely on your device.

Try it on the demo comic.

一路往下滑，字早就是中文了。

Otterflow 在 iPhone、iPad 與 Mac 上翻譯外文漫畫。打開漫畫頁面，按「翻譯閱讀」，一路往下滑：對話框的原文擦掉、用繁體中文重新嵌字，全部在你的裝置上完成。

可以用示範漫畫試試看。
